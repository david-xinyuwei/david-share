## Conclusion

At 255.375K context, output lengths up to 768 complete, while 1024 stalls under the strict stale rule. This narrows the generation-length failure threshold to **768-1024 generated tokens** for this context.

Together with the previous output-length diagnostic, this means:

- `output_len=1/64/256/512/768` succeed.
- `output_len=1024` repeatedly stales.
- The initial prefill-to-decode handoff and short/medium generation are viable.
- The failure appears in long decode generation, scheduler state, or streaming response-drain after several hundred generated tokens.
