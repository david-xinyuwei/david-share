- **three.js r160** ([MIT](https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/r160/LICENSE)) is not included. `tools/vendor_three.py` (or `studio/deploy/vendor_three.sh` on the VM) downloads the 15 files the studio uses from the npm package `three@0.160.0` and checks each against a pinned SHA-256.
- **Lucide icons** ([ISC](studio/static/icons/lucide-LICENSE)) are included as `studio/static/icons/lucide.min.js` with their license file.
- **CadQuery** and **cadquery-ocp** (both Apache-2.0; cadquery-ocp wraps the Open CASCADE Technology kernel), FastAPI (MIT), Uvicorn (BSD-3-Clause), the OpenAI Python library (Apache-2.0), azure-identity (MIT), Pydantic (MIT), python-docx (MIT) and their dependencies are installed with pip from `studio/requirements.txt`; none of them is redistributed here. `studio/app.py` also imports aiohttp (Apache-2.0 AND MIT), which `requirements.txt` does not list: it arrives with CadQuery's dependencies (via `wslink`). Licenses as declared in each package's metadata.
- **Microsoft Azure architecture icons V25** in `images/icons/` are used unmodified in the two diagrams under Microsoft's [icon terms](https://learn.microsoft.com/azure/architecture/icons/). `images/SOURCES.json` lists each file, its package entry and SHA-256.
- **The sedan and laptop models** in `studio/static/assets/` are generated by `studio/assets/build_car_shell.py` and `studio/assets/build_laptop.py` from our own CadQuery code; no third-party geometry.
- **The gpt-image pictures** in `images/` (`module-gpt-image-render.jpg` and `render-<scenario>.jpg`) were generated by the author with an Azure OpenAI gpt-image-2.5 deployment, each from a picture of a STEP solid; their request settings are recorded in `images/SOURCES.json`. The original PNGs carry C2PA content credentials, which these JPEG copies do not keep; the model origin of each picture is stated in `images/SOURCES.json` and next to the picture on every page that shows it.
- The architecture figure and the test-topology figure are original explanatory diagrams drawn by `tools/build_diagram.py` from `tools/architecture.html` and `tools/test-topology.html`.
