laptop14_open.glb / laptop14_closed.glb — generic 14-inch notebook, procedurally generated by assets/build_laptop.py (CadQuery / OCCT).
Own asset, no third-party geometry; free to use in customer-facing material.
Base 301 × 220 mm, 14.5 mm at the hinge tapering to 9.5 mm at the front; lid 301 × 212 × 4.2 mm, 3:2 display 288 × 192; open variant lid at 110°.
glTF keeps mm; root node rotated to Y-up. In three.js (1 unit = 1 mm): x = width ±150, y = up (base top y = 14.5, open lid up to y ≈ 215),
z = depth: palm rest / touchpad at +z (front, z ≈ +62), keyboard around z ≈ −30, hinge at z ≈ −104. No scaling needed.
Nodes (prefix match, one mesh per face): base*, keys*, touchpad*, hinge_l*, hinge_r*, lid*, screen*, feet*.
