Sharing a deep dive into DeepSeek-V4's post-training architecture — specifically how they replaced Mixed RL with On-Policy Distillation (OPD) to merge 10+ domain-specialist models into one production model. I verified the mechanism end-to-end on Azure H100.

Background: Enterprise customers with multiple fine-tuned models (math, code, writing, agent, etc.) face a consolidation problem — deploying N models means N× inference cost. DeepSeek-V4 solved this with OPD: distill all specialists into one model via logit-level alignment on the student's own trajectories.

Running on Azure: All 11 training runs and evaluations ran on Standard_NC80adis_H100_v5 (2× H100 NVL, 94 GB each). Single VM, no cluster required.

Results (Run 11, HumanEval 164 problems):

MetricBaselineOPDDelta
Greedy pass@122.56%26.22%+3.66 pp
Sampling pass@1 (mean of 10)18.72%23.48%+4.76 pp
pass@10 (production metric)49.39%55.49%+6.10 pp

SFT control experiment confirms that plain cross-domain SFT causes catastrophic forgetting (−5.49 pp greedy), while OPD improves both metrics. The repo includes 1900+ lines of theory, 11-run engineering trail, complete scripts, and cost breakdown.
